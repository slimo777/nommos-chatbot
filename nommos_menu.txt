NOMMOS — Restaurant Lounge, Tangier
Source: nommostanger.com (public menu, retrieved September 2026)

ABOUT
NOMMOS is a restaurant lounge on Tangier Bay with a view of the Mediterranean.
The decor is described as refined, blending tradition and modernity.
The kitchen serves Mediterranean and international cuisine, including steakhouse
meats and sushi. The venue hosts live cooking shows, live bands and DJ nights,
and takes bookings for private events.
